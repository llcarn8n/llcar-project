# Triggering alarm

The alarm will be triggered if:

• Any door, trunk lid or engine hood is unlocked without using the PEPS function of the intelligent key.   
• The vehicle is powered up by other means instead of the PEPS function of the intelligent key.

# Disarming alarm

The alarm can be disarmed by the following methods:

• Unlock doors or trunk lid with the inteligent key.   
• Unlock doors with the microswitch.   
• Unlock the trunk lid remotely with the electronic intelligent key.   
• Start the vehicle remotely with the key.

# Gearshift Actuator

R: reverse gear position

Shift to R position only after the vehicle is parked steadily.

N: neutral position

Shift to N position for stopping the vehicle for a short time.

D: drive gear position

Shift to D position to normally drive the vehicle.

![](images/bb9829e66db0c561b9b154aeb2cf22e99c804ae3253e36ddb72c14a81890c51f.webp)

After powering the vehicle up (OK), shift to D or R position and then the linkage with the EPB system will be achieved. Moreover, in D or R position, when the vehicle speed is ≤3 km/h and the driver door is

opened, the gearshift lever will automatically shift to P position for safety.

P: parking gear position

With the gearshift lever placed at this position, the motor for P position will be locked up and the switch indicator willight up. At this moment, the vehicle is shifted to P position.

Never place the gearshift lever to R position when the vehicle is driving forward. Be sure to place the gearshift lever to R position after confirming that the vehicle is completely stopped.

# Electric Parking Brake (EPB)

Manual parking: Pull up the EPB switch. The indicator on the instrument will flash first before staying on. If this indicator stays on, it indicates that the EPB has been applied.

Manual EPB releasing: When the vehicle has been powered up (OK) or started and

![](images/d94ee92e9293d4b8f7f21272efd9dee6594e93092e8561bde00192f23f394245.webp)

the vehicle is in a gear position other than P (parking) position, depress and keep holding the brake pedal and press the EPB switch to release the EPB until the indicator on the instrument is off, which indicates that the EPB has been released, and then the instrument will display the message of "EPB deactivated".

P position refers to the parking position. When the gearshiftlever is in P position, it is in a stable parking state. EPB is a main parking device of the vehicle. To ensure the vehicle's parking safety, releasing the EPB with the EPB switch can only be achieved when the gearshift lever is no in P (parking) position.

# Applying the EPB automatically

Applying the EPB automatically upon power-off:

• After stopping the vehicle by depressing the brake pedal, press the START/STOP button to stop the engine (the power mode is switched from ON to OFF), the EPB will be pulled up automatically. Release the brake pedal when the indicator () on

the instrument stays on after flashing and the message "EPB activated" is displayed.

Applying the EPB automatically when gearshift lever is in P position:

When the vehicle speed is less than 3 km/h, the gearshift lever is in D or R position and the door is being closed, the gearshift will shift to P position and the EPB will be applied automatically.

This function is designed to avoid adverse consequences caused by a driver leaving the vehicle after powering it off without applying parking brake, and to improve the vehicle safety. Excessive reliance on or frequent use of the function is not recommended.

# Releasing the EPB automatically when the vehicle is starting to move

Releasing the EPB automatically upon shifting gear:

• With the vehicle parked on a flat road, start the vehicle, depress and hold the brake pedal, and shift to D position or R position from P position or N position, and then the EPB will be released automatically, the indicator () will go out, and the instrument will display the message of "EPB deactivated".

Releasing the EPB automatically upon depressing accelerator pedal:

• When the vehicle has been started, the gearshift lever is in D or R position and the EPB is pulled up, depress the accelerator pedal slowly to a certain depth, and then the EPB will be released automatically, the indicator ) will go out, and the instrument will display the message of "EPB deactivated".

# EV mode

EV mode: When the EV mode button is pressed, the EV mode button indicator willight up, indicating that the vehicle is operating in the EV mode; if the indicator goes out a few seconds after lighting up, it indicates that the powertrain cannot enter the EV mode. In the EV mode, the vehicle is driven by the motors powered by the power battery.

Forced EV mode: To enter the forced EV mode, press and hold the EV mode button for more than 3s. When the EV indicator on the instrument flashes continuously, it indicates that the vehicle has run in the forced EV mode. The forced EV mode is designed to meet a user's need to drive at a low speed for a short trip with motor drive only. As this mode runs with insufficient SOC of the power battery, it is suggested that the vehicle be charged immediately after a trip using this mode.

![](images/a8072c1231451a114ef09aed08e7df363aee365d7b8eccf39e8a7e47215cb408.webp)

# HEV-ECO mode

HEV-ECO mode: Press the HEV mode button and turn the MODE knob to "ECO" to enter the HEV-ECO mode which can improve the driving economics of the vehicle.

# HEV-SPORT mode

HEV-SPORT mode: Press the HEV mode button and turn the MODE knob to SPORT to enter the HEV-SPORT mode. When the vehicle is operating in the HEV-SPORT mode, the best power performance can be ensured, but the economic efficiency of the vehicle is lower than that when it is operating in other modes. Therefore, driving the vehicle in this mode is not suggested in case of a traffic jam.

# Actively accelerating/decelerating with ACC activated

With the ACC activated, depress the accelerator pedal to accelerate the vehicle continuously. After the desired speed is reached, release the accelerator pedal. If the vehicle speed is higher than 150 km/h or the accelerator pedal is depressed continuously for more than 15 min, the system will enter the standby state, and you need to reactivate the ACC;

With the ACC activated, depress the brake pedal to decelerate the vehicle continuously. The system will automatically enter the standby state. After releasing the brake pedal, you need to reactivate the ACC.

# Vehicle following to stop/start

The ACC system can control the vehicle to follow a vehicle ahead to stop in normal driving condition. If the vehicle stops for less than 3s, the vehicle can follow a vehicle ahead to start;   
If the vehicle has stopped for less than 3 min, the driver needs to depress the accelerator pedal or operate the ACC lever to reactivate the ACC;   
If the vehicle has stopped for more than 3 min, the ACC system will enter the standby state and the EPB will be applied.

3. If conditions permit, perform a simple check on your own: check if the edge of the power battery tray is cracked and whether there is visible liquid flow.

### Gearshift Actuator

R: reverse gear position

Shift to R position only after the vehicle is parked steadily.

N: neutral position

Shift to N position for stopping the vehicle for a short time.

D: drive gear position

Shift to D position to normally drive the vehicle.

![](images/bb9829e66db0c561b9b154aeb2cf22e99c804ae3253e36ddb72c14a81890c51f.webp)

After powering the vehicle up (OK), shift to D or R position and then the linkage with the EPB system will be achieved. Moreover, in D or R position, when the vehicle speed is ≤3 km/h and the driver door is

opened, the gearshift lever will automatically shift to P position for safety.

P: parking gear position

With the gearshift lever placed at this position, the motor for P position will be locked up and the switch indicator willight up. At this moment, the vehicle is shifted to P position.

Never place the gearshift lever to R position when the vehicle is driving forward. Be sure to place the gearshift lever to R position after confirming that the vehicle is completely stopped.


### Electric Parking Brake (EPB)

Manual parking: Pull up the EPB switch. The indicator on the instrument will flash first before staying on. If this indicator stays on, it indicates that the EPB has been applied.

Manual EPB releasing: When the vehicle has been powered up (OK) or started and

![](images/d94ee92e9293d4b8f7f21272efd9dee6594e93092e8561bde00192f23f394245.webp)

the vehicle is in a gear position other than P (parking) position, depress and keep holding the brake pedal and press the EPB switch to release the EPB until the indicator on the instrument is off, which indicates that the EPB has been released, and then the instrument will display the message of "EPB deactivated".

P position refers to the parking position. When the gearshiftlever is in P position, it is in a stable parking state. EPB is a main parking device of the vehicle. To ensure the vehicle's parking safety, releasing the EPB with the EPB switch can only be achieved when the gearshift lever is no in P (parking) position.


### Applying the EPB automatically

Applying the EPB automatically upon power-off:

• After stopping the vehicle by depressing the brake pedal, press the START/STOP button to stop the engine (the power mode is switched from ON to OFF), the EPB will be pulled up automatically. Release the brake pedal when the indicator () on

the instrument stays on after flashing and the message "EPB activated" is displayed.

Applying the EPB automatically when gearshift lever is in P position:

When the vehicle speed is less than 3 km/h, the gearshift lever is in D or R position and the door is being closed, the gearshift will shift to P position and the EPB will be applied automatically.

This function is designed to avoid adverse consequences caused by a driver leaving the vehicle after powering it off without applying parking brake, and to improve the vehicle safety. Excessive reliance on or frequent use of the function is not recommended.


### Releasing the EPB automatically when the vehicle is starting to move

Releasing the EPB automatically upon shifting gear:

• With the vehicle parked on a flat road, start the vehicle, depress and hold the brake pedal, and shift to D position or R position from P position or N position, and then the EPB will be released automatically, the indicator () will go out, and the instrument will display the message of "EPB deactivated".

Releasing the EPB automatically upon depressing accelerator pedal:

• When the vehicle has been started, the gearshift lever is in D or R position and the EPB is pulled up, depress the accelerator pedal slowly to a certain depth, and then the EPB will be released automatically, the indicator ) will go out, and the instrument will display the message of "EPB deactivated".

### EV mode

EV mode: When the EV mode button is pressed, the EV mode button indicator willight up, indicating that the vehicle is operating in the EV mode; if the indicator goes out a few seconds after lighting up, it indicates that the powertrain cannot enter the EV mode. In the EV mode, the vehicle is driven by the motors powered by the power battery.

Forced EV mode: To enter the forced EV mode, press and hold the EV mode button for more than 3s. When the EV indicator on the instrument flashes continuously, it indicates that the vehicle has run in the forced EV mode. The forced EV mode is designed to meet a user's need to drive at a low speed for a short trip with motor drive only. As this mode runs with insufficient SOC of the power battery, it is suggested that the vehicle be charged immediately after a trip using this mode.

### Vehicle flood

If the pure electric car is immersed in deep water (about 400 mm), operate vehicle as follows

Please de-energize the vehicle to the "OFF" gear and disconnect the front-cabin start-up type iron battery if possible. Under the conditions of insulation protection, remove the vehicle from the water and open

the door, and drain water inside vehicle;

Pull out service switch;

Call the BYD authorized service shop for help.


### Towing of Vehicle

For towing the vehicle, contact a BYD authorized service provider. Never have your vehicle towed by another vehicle using ropes or iron chains only.

Most common towing methods:

Flatbed device – The operator will load your vehicle onto a truck. This is the best way to tow your vehicle.


### Flat Tyre

Be sure to gradually slow down the vehicle and keep the vehicle running in a straight line. Slowly drive the vehicle away from the crowded traffic to a safe place.

Turn off the vehicle and turn on the hazard warning lights.

Pull up the EPB switch and press the "P" button.

Be sure to have all passengers get off the vehicle and ask them to go to a safe place away from crowded traffic.

Confirm if any tyre is flat. For slow leaking, fix the tyre with tyre repair kit and tyre puncture sealant. After tyre fixing, the vehicle running speed should be lower than 80 km/h.
